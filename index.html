How it works.

Brandon Trinh and Sai Uppuluri co-founded Helping Hands. Our lead tutor covered all three AP subjects, wrote our two-page tutor guide, and trained three younger tutors.

  1. Start with your class

    Tell us your course and teacher. We use both to look for a peer who’s already done well in that classroom.

  2. Bring a question each week

    Work through difficult concepts with your tutor. Use targeted worksheets written for the topics you want to practice.

  3. Get ready for exam day

    Try a mock exam and revisit the questions that trip you up at a pre-exam review workshop.
